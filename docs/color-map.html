TUI Color Map

The TUI always uses the same set of eight colors, applied in different places with different meanings depending on context. This page collects every place where they are still used in the current TUI.

"Tor"/"I2P" tab status bar

Tor: ● out ● in, H.S.:, make up the bar at the top of the network tab (labeled "Tor" or "I2P" depending on the network chosen at startup). By the time the interface starts, the hidden service is already published, so H.S. tracks the outbound circuit itself: ◌ … before the first circuit check, ● green once a circuit is active, ✗ red if the circuit is later lost.

OutInH.S.Description
red9 red9 gray8 Startup — no circuit check completed yet (H.S. shows ◌ …)
green10 red9 green10 Outbound circuit active, inbound (reachability of your own hidden service) not yet confirmed
green10 green10 green10 Outbound and inbound both confirmed
red9 red9 red9 Circuits lost after being active — outbound and inbound reset (H.S. shows ✗, distinct from the gray startup state above)

Contact List

Each contact row carries two independent reachability markers — neither is derived from the other, so any combination of the two can appear (e.g. a contact can show as unreachable on marker 1 while marker 2 is still green, if their last probe_ack arrived before the connection to them broke).

ColorMarker 1 — do I reach them?
gray8 ◌ — never probed, or outcome unknown
green10 ● — reachable
red9 ✗ — not reachable

Marker 2, independent of marker 1:

ColorMarker 2 — do they reach me?
gray8 ◌ — unknown, no probe_ack from them yet
green10 ● — reciprocity confirmed, they replied with a probe_ack
red9 ✗ — no confirmation ever arrived despite reaching them continuously for several probe cycles; not a diagnosis (indistinguishable from being removed from their address book, our own probe never landing, or a one-way NAT/firewall issue — the protocol deliberately doesn't reveal which)

Next, on the same line as the contact, comes the relationship key tag:

ColorElementMeaning
green10 [REL:C64130FC] Relationship key (ContentKey) established in both directions with this contact
gray8 [REL:········] Relationship key exchange not yet completed with this contact

The address book groups contacts by trust level; each group heading is colored. A contact whose relationship keys no longer match what was previously known (a potential key-substitution attack, requiring re-verification) is pulled into a dedicated group shown first, above every other group:

ColorGroup headingMeaning
red9 KEY CONFLICT Relationship-key mismatch detected — shown first regardless of the contact's prior trust level, since this is an urgency signal, not a trust ranking
green10 VERIFIED & INTRODUCED Verified in person, introduced by another contact
green10 VERIFIED CONTACTS Verified in person, added directly
yellow11 INTRODUCED CONTACTS (UNVERIFIED) Introduced by another contact, not yet verified in person
red9 UNVERIFIED Not yet verified, added directly — here the contact is in the address book, just unverified

Chat Messages

Each message line carries up to three independent tags in brackets — signature (REL), device (DEV), post-quantum (PQC) — plus the ACK marker. The REL tag communicates two things at once: what the individual message's signature proves, and whether the relationship with that sender is a saved, persisted one or only born from the room's ephemeral bootstrap (sender not among saved contacts).

ColorElementMeaning
cyan14 alice: Sender name when the message is yours
green10 bob: Sender name when the message is from another user
gray8 [no signature] No content signature present on the message
gray8 [REL:........] Signature present but no ContentKey candidate known yet for the sender (neither persisted nor ephemeral) — verification not yet possible
gray8 [REL:c64130fc] Valid signature, verified against a persisted ContentKey (saved contact with an established address-book relationship)
yellow11 [REL:c64130fc] Valid signature, but verified against an ephemeral ContentKey (born only from the room's bootstrap, sender not among saved contacts) — same coloring as Android (room_tab.go, theme.ColorNameWarning)
red9 [signature invalid] At least one ContentKey candidate is known, but none of them verifies the message's signature
gray8 [your message] Your own messages: no meaningful self-verification (a per-recipient signature, never a single "your own" ContentKey to show)
gray8 [DEV:........] Device tag, always present (never omitted, same line format for every message) — placeholder while the sender's device identifier isn't known yet (never on your own messages: your own device is always known)
gray8 [DEV:a1b2c3d4] Known device identifier, unchanged from the last message seen from this sender
yellow11 [DEV:a1b2c3d4] Device identifier just changed from the last message seen from this sender (key rotation or new device)
red9 [PQC] Message without the additional ML-DSA-65 signature (post-quantum protection off for this message)
green10 [PQC] Message also protected by an additional ML-DSA-65 signature
gray8 ○ Your own broadcast message, no peer has confirmed receipt yet
green10 ✓3 Number of peers who confirmed receipt
